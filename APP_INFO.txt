 FULL DESCRIPTION - 4,000 characters max

AC Ustad is the offline error-code guide for air-conditioner and
solar-inverter technicians. When a unit shows a code like E6, F4, Error 200,
ID013, or a blinking LED, AC Ustad tells you what it means, what is causing it,
and how to fix it - in plain English or Roman Urdu, without needing internet.

No signal? No problem.
The whole knowledge base lives on your phone: 62 brands, 320 model lines and
4,418 error codes. Open the app in airplane mode and every answer is still
right there. No account, no sign-up, no waiting for a server.

The right answer for the exact unit
The same code can mean different things on different machines. AC Ustad always
looks at the brand, model line and code together, so you get the answer for the
unit in front of you - not a generic guess.

Every code gives you:
- A clear severity label: DANGER, STOP, CHECK, CLEARS, INFO or INDICATOR
- What the code means, in everyday words
- Numbered fix steps you can follow on site
- Likely causes to check first
- A source reference from the service manual

English or Roman Urdu - your choice
Switch the whole app in one tap. Meanings, fix steps, causes and severity words
all change together, and the app remembers your choice offline.

Search fast. Save what you use.
Search brands, model lines and codes from any screen, with real technician
words like "air leakage" or "water pump". Star the codes you use most and find
them in the Saved tab. Swipe to remove, with undo if you change your mind.

Your data stays yours
No account. No analytics. No crash reporting. No tracking beyond the ads that
keep the app free. Your saved codes and settings stay on your phone. The
knowledge base never needs the network: in airplane mode the ad slots are
simply empty, and every code still works.

Why it is free
AC Ustad is supported by Google AdMob. Ad networks may see your device and IP
address when an ad loads. The app asks for consent where required and requests
no runtime permissions.

Coverage
Air conditioners and solar inverters from 62 brands used across Pakistan and
the region - split ACs, inverter ACs, VRF systems and hybrid solar inverters.
Every code comes from manufacturer manuals and service documentation.

Made for the people who fix things
AC technicians, solar installers, service engineers, shopkeepers, and anyone
who needs a straight answer from a fault code without carrying a folder of
manuals.

Download AC Ustad today and keep the answer in your pocket - even when the
signal is not.
