Privacy policy — AC Ustad

Last updated: 2026-11-10.

AC Ustad is an offline reference for air conditioner and solar inverter error codes. This policy says what the app does with your information, in plain words.

The short version

  • The app itself has no account, no sign-in, and no tracking of its own.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob to stay free. AdMob collects some data and shares it with Google to pick and measure those ads.
  • Everything you do inside the app — saved codes, language, theme — stays on your phone.

What we collect

We do not run a server and we do not have an account system, so we (AC Ustad) do not collect or store any personal data ourselves.

What Google AdMob collects and shares

When an ad loads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the following and shares it with Google for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

DataWhat it is
Approximate locationDerived from your device's IP address. Not GPS, and the app never asks for a location permission.
App interactionsApp launch, taps and video views inside the app, used to measure ad performance.
DiagnosticsApp and SDK performance such as launch time, hang rate and energy usage.
Device or other IDsYour Android advertising ID and app set ID.

All of it is encrypted in transit with TLS. We never receive this data; it goes from your device to Google. Google's handling of it is covered by Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Advertising

The app shows ads to stay free, through Google AdMob. The knowledge base itself — every code, meaning and fix step — never needs the network: airplane mode answers everything, with the ad slots simply empty.

You can control ad personalisation at any time:

  • Reset or delete your advertising ID: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Opt out of personalised ads: the same screen, "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalisation".
  • Google's ad settings: adssettings.google.com

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change that choice later from the same consent screen.

What stays on your phone

  • Codes you star, kept in the Saved tab.
  • Your language (English / Roman Urdu) and theme choices.
  • A copy of the bundled knowledge base, stored in the app's own cache.

All of it lives on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Permissions

The app uses only what its ads need — network access, the Advertising ID, the Privacy Sandbox ad APIs (Topics, attribution) and two install-time permissions the ad processes need. None of them prompts at runtime, and nothing else is requested. There is no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permission.

Source links

26 codes carry no source link and show none. Where a source link is shown, tapping it opens the page in your own browser. The app never opens a page on its own and never fetches anything in the background.

Third parties

The only third party that receives data through this app is Google AdMob, as described above. No data is sold, and no data goes to any other company.

The app is built with AndroidX, Kotlin and kotlinx.coroutines (Apache License 2.0) and uses IBM Plex type (SIL Open Font License 1.1). Those are code libraries on your phone; they receive nothing from you.

Data deletion

The app has no account and stores nothing on a server, so there is no server-side data to delete. Codes you saved, your language and your theme live only on your phone: uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings to remove them.

To reset or delete the advertising ID used for ads, open Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

To ask a privacy question or request deletion, email atif.support@proton.me. We answer within 30 days.

Children

The app is a professional reference for technicians and is not directed at children. It is not offered in Google Play's Designed for Families programme.

Contact

The data version shown in Settings tells you whether the answers on your phone are current. For any privacy request, use the email above.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the in-app Privacy panel is rewritten in the same update, before anything new arrives.
