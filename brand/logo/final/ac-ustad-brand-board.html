Final design

The Compass

A C-shaped dial and needle: the right answer, even when the signal is gone.

  • The C is the C in AC; the open ring reads as a field gauge.
  • The orange needle points to the answer — a diagnostic tool, not a snowflake.
  • The mark is built on a 256 grid and survives 16 px.
  • The blue tile + white mark makes a strong app icon.
