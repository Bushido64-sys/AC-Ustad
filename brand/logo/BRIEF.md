# Design brief — AC Ustad

**Summary**: An offline error-code reference for AC and solar-inverter technicians in Pakistan — the "ustad" (master/teacher) in your pocket when a unit throws a code and there is no signal.

**Audience**: AC technicians, solar installers, service engineers, shopkeepers; roughly 20–45; working on phones outdoors, often in sunlight, sometimes with dusty or greasy hands. They meet the mark as an app icon, on WhatsApp, on a workshop wall, and on a shirt or cap.

**Brand adjectives**: trustworthy, practical, expert, friendly, local, clear.
**Avoid feeling**: corporate, toy-like, cold, generic tech.

**Promise / big idea**: The right answer for the exact unit — even offline.

**Competitive landscape**: The category is full of snowflakes, AC-unit illustrations, wrenches, gauges, gears, lightning bolts and blue gradients. The open territory is a human, mentor-like "Ustad" identity: a master technician's mark of approval, not another tool icon.

**Mark type hypothesis**: Combination mark — a compact letterform/monogram symbol (works as the app icon) plus a wordmark. The symbol carries "Ustad" + "verified answer"; the wordmark carries the name.

**Must work on**: app icon 48/192/512, favicon, WhatsApp avatar, one-colour print, embroidery, dark mode, horizontal and stacked lockups.

**Constraints**: use the app's blue `#1668A8` and signal orange `#E24A1E`; no gradients; no fine details; no live text in final; the old snowflake/AC-unit icon is not equity to preserve.

**Assumptions**: the name is final; English + Roman Urdu; free/ad-supported; the decision-maker is the user.

## Word map

- **Name**: AC (air conditioner) + Ustad (master, teacher).
- **Offering**: error codes, meanings, causes, fixes, offline.
- **Adjectives**: trustworthy, practical, expert, friendly, local, clear, rugged.
- **Promise**: "the right answer, even offline"; "fix it right the first time"; "a master technician in your pocket."
- **Nouns / metaphors**: master, teacher, guide, answer key, check mark, badge, stamp, toolbox, manual, compass.
- **Opposites**: guesswork, confusion, internet dependency, generic.
- **Intersections**: master + verified answer → check/badge; guide + offline → key/compass; local + expert → stamp/seal.

## Category clichés to avoid

Snowflake, AC unit, thermometer, wrench, gear, lightning bolt, blue gradient, circuit lines, generic gauge.

## Open territory

A human, mentor-like mark of approval: the "Ustad" idea expressed through a letterform or a master's stamp, not a literal tool.
